Legal Framework for Establishing Associations

National Portal for Remote Association Training — Morocco — May 12, 2026

View full-size image

About this certificate

A certified module explaining Morocco's complete legal framework for associations from A to Z: how to legally found an association, required documents, governing bodies and their roles, rights and obligations, and a sound relationship with the administration — from preparing the file to the association's daily legal management.

Skills you gain

  • Founding associations in Morocco
  • Preparing legal files
  • Governing bodies and their roles
  • Legal rights and obligations
  • Sound legal management of associations

Prospects & applications

Practically enables taking part in founding and running associations in full legal compliance — a sought-after skill in community work, civic initiatives and institutional partnerships.

Related paths & roles

  • Association founder or board member
  • Administrative & legal support
  • Community initiatives
