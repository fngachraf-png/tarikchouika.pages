About this certificate

A training module focused on the heart of successful community work: communication. It covers internal communication between association members, external communication with the public, partners and institutions, and organizing messages and field campaigns that amplify the association's impact and widen its base of support.

Skills you gain

  • Planning community communication
  • Communicating with the public & partners
  • Organizing field awareness campaigns
  • Building clear, compelling messages
  • Organizing teamwork

Prospects & applications

Strengthens readiness for coordination and communication roles in any collective effort — inside associations or beyond — where communication remains the most demanded skill in every successful team.

Related paths & roles

  • Communication coordinator
  • Organizing team member
  • Community facilitator
