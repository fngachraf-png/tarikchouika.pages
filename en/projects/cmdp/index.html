CMDP — Cooperative Management Platform

A comprehensive Moroccan platform that digitizes cooperative governance — members, general assemblies, boards, finances and documents — with supervisory-authority tracking and Law 112.12 compliance, in a modern zellige-inspired design.

Visit the live site

About this project

CMDP is a digital platform for managing Moroccan cooperatives, designed around the daily reality of a cooperative: eight interconnected units covering a dashboard with instant indicators and charts, cooperative cards with local-registry registration and the ODCO card, a members-and-shares registry with board positions, general assemblies with minutes and board meetings, income, expenses and ready financial reports, a time-stamped document registry with expiry alerts, tracking of interactions with ODCO, taxes and CNSS, and a legal library gathering Law 112.12, its decrees and principles — all in a fully Arabic RTL interface with a modern Moroccan design rooted in zellige and the Moroccan arch.

The effort behind the build

The biggest challenge was translating dry legal texts into a clear user experience: I built eight interconnected units following the rhythm of the cooperative year — from founding to the annual general assembly — making sure each unit works alone and with the others. I also set the national reference figures (65,315 active cooperatives, over 788,000 members, 34% women, 40,033 agricultural cooperatives) to place each cooperative’s management within its true national picture.

Tools & skills — explained simply

Every tool, library and skill I used in this project, explained in plain language: what it is and what it specifically added — no cryptic symbols.

Static frontend

Light pages served from Cloudflare’s global network: fast loading in Morocco and beyond, with instant updates on every new release — no expensive servers.

Local SQLite database

A tiny serverless data engine that stores cooperatives, members and transactions in a single file — ideal given the sensitivity of cooperative data.

Modern Moroccan design

The spirit of zellige and the Moroccan arch in a digital interface: a local visual identity in a contemporary language that sets the platform apart from generic templates.

Dashboard & charts

Instant indicators and charts showing season income, growth rate and assembly dates — decisions based on numbers, not impressions.

Eight-unit architecture

Every function (members, finance, documents...) is a standalone unit that works alone and integrates with the rest — development moves forward without breaking the whole.

Law 112.12 library

Legal texts and decrees organized and linked to actions — from founding with five members to the ordinary annual general assembly.

Expiry alerts

Documents that expire (registration, renewal...) alert you ahead of time — the cooperative is never surprised by an expired paper when it matters.

Native Arabic RTL

Designed right-to-left from the very first line, not a mirrored translation — a natural experience for the Moroccan user.

What I learned from it

CMDP taught me to turn “bureaucracy” from an enemy into design material: Law 112.12 is not an obstacle but a ready-made functional map. I learned to read regulatory text and extract the workflows — from assembly quorum to renewal deadlines — then turn them into simple screens. It also deepened my conviction that digitalization in Morocco succeeds when it respects local context: language, visual identity and the real world of cooperatives.
