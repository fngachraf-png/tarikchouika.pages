About this project

NATSUP is a digital guide and store specialized in natural supplements, built to serve health and fitness enthusiasts: it offers clear, simplified supplement reviews — ingredients, benefits and prices — with trusted official purchase links for every product. The interface is multilingual and covers well-known global products, with one goal: making the buying decision informed, safe and deliberate.

Technologies used

  • HTML5
  • CSS3
  • JavaScript
  • Multilingual translation engine
  • Structured JSON data layer
  • SEO optimization
  • Cloudflare Pages

Key features

  • Product catalog with images, ingredients and prices
  • Trusted official purchase links
  • Multilingual interface
  • Organized educational content
  • Mobile-friendly design

What I learned from it

This project taught me how a large, organized content system is built — data, translation and presentation — how purchase and tracking links are managed cleanly, and how clarity and honesty build visitor trust.
