About Me

Internationally certified digital knowledge, on top of a solid base of ten years in the field

I am Tarik Chouika, a Moroccan professional from Rabat who chose to redraw his career path with confidence toward the digital world. My knowledge is documented by 13 certified credentials from Google, HP LIFE, Huawei and national training institutions, covering artificial intelligence, digital marketing, web technologies, leadership and professional communication — knowledge I develop and apply every day, not certificates hanging on a wall.

My difference is that I don't just learn; I build. I put what I gained into four live digital projects developed with the help of AI models: a multilingual natural-supplements guide and store, a complete Arabic e-commerce store, a platform for traditional Moroccan games with live multiplayer rooms, and an HR platform built around the Moroccan labor code — from idea to deployment on Cloudflare Pages.

Before this digital journey, I spent more than ten years in fields that tolerate no laziness: running and operating a popular café with full responsibility for operations, inventory and service, alongside experience in construction and security guarding. That is where my skills were truly forged: customer service, pressure management, discipline, and reading people.

Today I offer this rare blend: a solid field mindset together with verifiable, certified digital knowledge. I am looking for an opportunity to prove that my certificates are not just paper, and that a decade of serving the public builds a professional who never lets you down. Ready to start immediately.
